# Other CV versions

Older or alternative versions of your CV. Anyone who has been job hunting has
several — an "operations" one and a "programme" one of the same career, worded
differently, often containing a project one had room for and the other did not.

Those differences are useful. They are your own words about your own work,
already checked by you, which makes them a better source than anything scraped
from a profile page.

## These are reference material, not your CV

`cv.md` in the project root is the canonical one. It is what roles are scored
against, and what the source-of-truth rules treat as the record of what you
claim. Everything in this folder is reference: something a suggestion can be
drawn from, never a fact that reaches a CV on its own.

That distinction is why these are never merged automatically. An older version
may word a job title differently, quote a figure you later revised, or list a
role you deliberately stopped mentioning. Merging them silently would bring
back things you removed on purpose and leave two sources disagreeing about your
own history — so anything found here is offered to you as a suggestion, and only
enters `cv.md` when you say so.

## Adding them

Through **My details** in the app, or by dropping `.md` files in here directly.
Files are named `01-the-ops-one.md` and so on; the name is only a label for you.

Nothing in this folder is committed to git — same as `writing-samples/`, and for
the same reason.
